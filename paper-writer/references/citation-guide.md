# Citation Verification Guide

Use this guide when adding or repairing citations in an English paper.

## Verify Before Adding

For every new entry, record a source that establishes its title, authors, year, venue or archive, and persistent identifier when one exists. Prefer the publisher, proceedings, DOI registry, author-maintained paper page, or the supplied PDF. Copy BibTeX from a verified record where possible, then check its fields against that record.

Check the local claim as well as the metadata. A citation may establish a method, dataset, benchmark, result, limitation, or contrast; write that relationship accurately. Do not cite a paper merely because its title resembles the claim.

## Audit Existing Sources

Check for unresolved cite keys, duplicate entries, inconsistent metadata, unused bibliography entries that affect the target build, and citations that do not support their surrounding text. Preserve an explicit placeholder for a needed but unverified source rather than fabricating an entry.

## Integration Record

When the task needs an auditable review, use this compact record:

| Claim or use | Cite key | Verified source | Relationship checked | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|

The record is a working artifact and need not be included in the manuscript unless the user requests it.
