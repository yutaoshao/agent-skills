# Literature Review

Use this module to find, verify, compare, or integrate English CS/ML/AI literature.

## Search From the Paper's Actual Question

Extract the problem, setting, technical mechanism, evaluation measures, and intended comparisons from the paper materials. For each, form queries with the field term, variants, adjacent terms, and a limiting term that expresses the setting. Search across appropriate primary indexes or publisher records, such as proceedings archives, DBLP, ACL Anthology, arXiv, ACM DL, IEEE Xplore, or the supplied bibliography. Use seed papers for backward and forward citation tracing when that is more informative than broad keyword search.

Search for work that can establish the problem, define a competing family, provide a canonical method or dataset, or challenge the proposed positioning. Do not treat popularity, citation count, or recency as evidence of relevance. Record unverified candidates separately rather than converting them into citations.

## Verify and Classify

For each source that may enter the manuscript, verify its metadata from a primary record or supplied source and identify what it supports. Classify it by its relationship to the paper, for example:

| Role | Question it answers |
|---|---|
| Problem or foundation | Why is this task or definition established? |
| Method family | What approaches solve the task and under what assumptions? |
| Comparator or baseline | What is a fair alternative in the same setting? |
| Dataset or benchmark | What establishes the evaluation protocol? |
| Limitation or contrast | Which tradeoff or boundary distinguishes the work? |

Do not infer a limitation from a title or abstract alone. Attribute a result, scope, or weakness only to what the source supports. Read [citation-guide.md](../references/citation-guide.md) before creating or repairing BibTeX.

## Synthesize Rather Than Inventory

Organize related work around the comparison the reader needs to understand. A useful paragraph typically introduces a method family or problem view, explains the relevant capability and tradeoff across several sources, then identifies the precise open distinction that leads to the present work. The resulting gap must be supported by the comparison, not asserted because the paper needs novelty.

End the section by positioning the present work against the closest alternatives: specify shared setting, material difference, and the evidence that will test that difference. Preserve placeholder citations for missing primary verification.
