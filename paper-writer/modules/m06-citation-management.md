# Citation Management

Use this module to add, verify, correct, or audit citations and BibTeX.

Verify bibliographic metadata from a primary record or supplied source before adding an entry. Check that the cited work actually supports the nearby statement, every cite key resolves, and duplicate or conflicting entries are handled deliberately. Integrate citations as part of the argument rather than as an unexamined cluster.

Do not use reference-count, recency, or per-section quotas. A missing source remains visibly unresolved until it can be verified. Read [citation-guide.md](../references/citation-guide.md) for the verification record and repair steps.
