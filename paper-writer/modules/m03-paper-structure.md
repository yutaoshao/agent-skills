# Paper Structure

Use this module to turn research material into an outline or repair a manuscript's argument.

Identify the central claim and map its dependencies: the problem and gap, the technical mechanism, the evaluation needed to test it, and the resulting scope. Build sections around the questions a skeptical reader must be able to answer. An outline may include an abstract, introduction, related work, method, experiments, limitations, and conclusion when they serve the paper and target venue; do not force a universal section layout.

For each major section, record its reader question, supported claim, necessary evidence, and connection to the next section. Flag claims that lack an experiment, derivation, source, or qualified wording.
