# Review and Submission Preparation

Use this module for a manuscript-level review or target-venue submission package.

Evaluate whether each material claim has appropriate evidence, the method and experiments can be followed, figures and tables agree with source results, citations resolve and support their statements, and the rendered PDF meets the supplied template. Report findings by severity with their location, evidence, and a concrete repair.

For a named venue, obtain the current official requirements before declaring readiness. Verify only requirements that apply to the paper, such as page limits, anonymization, supplementary material, reproducibility, ethics, disclosures, and submission fields. Read [evidence-review.md](../references/evidence-review.md) and [latex-checklist.md](../references/latex-checklist.md).
