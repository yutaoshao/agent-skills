# Figures and Tables

Use this module to create, edit, or audit visual evidence in an English paper.

For each figure or table, identify the source data, transformation or aggregation, units, rounding, and the claim it is meant to support. Compare displayed values against their source and report exact mismatches by cell, series, caption, or cited result. Check that labels, legends, notation, captions, and text use the same terms and units.

Design the visual for its evidence task and the target template. Use accessible distinctions and readable text at the rendered size. Do not claim a figure or table is publication-ready until the source and rendered output have both been inspected when they are available. Read [evidence-review.md](../references/evidence-review.md) for the audit record and full review scope.
