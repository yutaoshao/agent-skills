# English Prose Revision

Use this module to improve clarity, cohesion, and academic precision in an English manuscript.

Edit paragraphs according to their argument: make the claim, evidence, comparison, limitation, or transition explicit where needed. Remove repetition, vague intensifiers, and unsupported conclusions when they obscure meaning. Treat recurring language patterns as prompts for review, not prohibited words; a word is acceptable when it conveys the intended scientific relationship.

Preserve technical meaning, citations, notation, equations, and LaTeX commands. Re-read each edited passage with its surrounding context and flag any statement whose evidence, referent, or scope remains unclear.
